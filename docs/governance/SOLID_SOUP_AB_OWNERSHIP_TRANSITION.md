## Intended legal rights holder
**Solid Soup AB** is the intended legal rights holder and approving entity for
future development, licensing and publication of the Solid Soup software
portfolio. The GitHub organization `Solid-soup` is a technical account name,
not a separate legal person and not by itself proof of title.

**Status: PENDING DOCUMENTED CONFIRMATION AND, WHERE REQUIRED, ASSIGNMENT.**
Do not interpret this statement as a completed transfer, a change to historic
release approvals, or proof that every contributor's rights have vested in
Solid Soup AB. Do not invent a corporate registration number or effective date.

## Required evidence before G1 may be recorded as Solid Soup AB ownership
1. Verify the company's registered legal name, organization number and
   authorized signatory.
2. Prepare a chain-of-title inventory: original contributors, employment or
   contractor arrangements, code created before incorporation, prior legal
   entities, external libraries, and any IP encumbrances or third-party
   licences. Exclude material that cannot lawfully be assigned.
3. Obtain signed assignments or other legal basis for transfers from each
   relevant current rights holder to Solid Soup AB, identifying the assets,
   consideration where relevant, effective date, warranties and exclusions.
   Retain the executed agreements privately, not in this public repository.
4. Review contributor agreements and ensure future employee/contractor
   contributions vest in Solid Soup AB under appropriate written terms.
5. Confirm the GitHub organization has at least two company-authorized owners
   and company-controlled recovery/security settings; review billing,
   deploy keys, tokens, integrations and transfer/revocation permissions.
6. Have an authorized representative and counsel confirm the legal ownership
   evidence before updating G1 or the next release's owner approval.
   Record only a non-confidential sign-off reference in release governance.

## Release and open-source boundaries
- Apache-2.0 remains unchanged for already licensed contributions.
- Previously granted open-source licences are not retroactively revoked
  through a change in legal ownership.
- No automatic change to public repository visibility, package ownership
  at distribution indexes, release tags or contributor copyright notices.
- Keep `Private :: Do Not Upload` and the version-specific D6/G7 owner
  approval requirement for the next package release.
- G3 IP review must confirm no proprietary Solid Soup source or customer data
  appears in public repositories. Legal ownership is not permission to publish
  proprietary code.
